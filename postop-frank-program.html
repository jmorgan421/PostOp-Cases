DPHT 7441 · Module 6 · Sync Session · Branching case

Build Frank's Program

Right total hip arthroplasty, posterolateral approach · three phases, three gates
How this works. You are designing Frank's rehabilitation program one phase at a time, from maximum protection to return to activity. At each phase you choose what goes in and what has to be true before he moves on. The case then fast-forwards and shows you what your choice did. You can work with a group or on your own. Choices lock once made. A detour shows you where a choice leads, then returns you to the main path. The surgical report and the surgeon's protocol are one click away at every decision.
