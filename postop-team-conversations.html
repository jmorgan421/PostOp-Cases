Four Conversations, Four Patients

Scene 1: Dolores, knee · Scene 2: Tessa, hip · Scene 3: Darnell, lumbar spine · Scene 4: Jordan, foot and ankle
How this works. Four scenes, one patient per region, played in order: Scene 1 Dolores (knee), Scene 2 Tessa (hip), Scene 3 Darnell (lumbar spine), Scene 4 Jordan (foot and ankle). Every scene is a call with the surgeon, then back to the patient. Read the step-by-step guide below before you start; it explains exactly what to do in your role.
How to use this activity, step by step

Before you start (once)

  1. Everyone opens this page on their own device.
  2. Decide who is Person A, B, C, and D. Alphabetical by first name works. Your letter tells you your role in every scene; each scene's brief lists it.
  3. Play the scenes in order: Scene 1 Dolores, Scene 2 Tessa, Scene 3 Darnell, Scene 4 Jordan. In a group of four, everyone plays the physical therapist once. In a group of three, Person A plays the physical therapist again in Scene 4.

Every scene, in this order

  1. Open the scene tab and read the shared brief together, out loud if that helps.
  2. Find your role in the brief's Roles line. Under Choose your role, tap only your own role. Do not open the other roles, and do not share your screen if you are the surgeon or the patient: those cards hold information the physical therapist should not see.
  3. The timer keeper taps Start on the scene timer below the brief. The timer keeper is the observer: Person D (Person A in Scene 4). In a group of three, the surgeon player is the timer keeper, including in Scene 1, where Person C switches to playing Dolores. When the timer turns amber, the timer keeper says the next step out loud and taps Next step. Everyone else can ignore the timer.
  4. Part 1 and Part 2 run as the brief describes: Part 1 is the physical therapist and the surgeon; Part 2 is the physical therapist back with the patient while the surgeon listens.
  5. Read-out: the surgeon and the patient read their conversation paths aloud; the observer reads what never happened.
  6. Reflection: one person taps We played the scene: open the reflection, and you answer the reflection questions together.
  7. Rotate roles and open the next scene tab.

Your role, step by step

Physical therapist

Before: read your card. It holds only what would be in the chart. Write your plan in the six boxes, in your own words; there are no hints on purpose. Tap Show my card to keep your plan in front of you.

During: talk. There is no script. Glance at your card if you need it.

After: once the reflection is open, tap Review my plan for feedback on what you planned.

Surgeon

Before: read your card: who you are and how you react. If your screen shows Start the scene clock, tap it when the scene begins.

During Part 1: listen. When the physical therapist does something on your list under What did the PT just do?, tap it and say the line that appears, in your own words. React only to what they actually say. Do not rescue them.

During Part 2: stay on, stay quiet, and listen.

After: read your conversation path aloud, then tell the group whether the patient heard the same message you gave.

Patient

Before: read your card. The Held back box is your secret: keep it unless the physical therapist earns it with the right question. If your screen shows Start the scene clock, tap it when the scene begins.

During: when the physical therapist does something on your list, tap it and say your line. When they ask the question that earns your secret, tell them and tap The PT asked. I told them.

After: read your conversation path aloud, then say what you were holding back and whether it came out.

Observer (and timer keeper)

Person D, or Person A in Scene 4. In a group of three there is no observer; the surgeon player keeps the timer.

Before: tap Start on the scene timer, then Start the scene clock in your checklist.

During: tap each checklist item the moment it happens; the time is stamped for you. Jot notes. When the timer turns amber, say the next step out loud and tap Next step.

After: tap End of scene: show my summary and read what never happened first.

Scene 1 only: Person C observes the call in Part 1, then switches roles for Part 2: tap Dolores, patient (Part 2) under Choose your role. Scene 3 only: the patient, Darnell, is on the call in Part 1. Nothing you do here is saved, submitted, or graded.
